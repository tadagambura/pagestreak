# PageStreak

**Read a little every day**

> #7 of 33 · Wave 1: Daily keepers · Used: Daily · Easy · Revenue potential: Medium · Habits, Health & Learning

## What it is

Log the pages or minutes you read each day, keep a reading streak, track your books and a yearly goal, and get a shareable reading recap. Duolingo-style streaks applied to reading.

## Why it's in the portfolio

BookTok and Bookstagram are huge free distribution channels, and readers love sharing their stats. A daily streak gives strong retention with very simple code.

## Who it's for

Readers who want to read more, especially anyone with a yearly book goal.

**The problem:** People want to read more but lose the habit, and most reading apps are social networks first.

## Version one

- Log pages or minutes in one tap
- Daily streak with freezes
- Currently reading list and a yearly goal
- Monthly and yearly recap cards
- Reminder at the user's reading time

**Example:** Aiming for 24 books this year, a user logs 15 pages before bed, keeps a 40-day streak, and shares their monthly recap.

## Later

- Reading challenges
- Stats and themes

## Animation and tone: five signature animations

**Playful.** Celebrations, confetti, cute characters, satisfying sounds and mild comic reactions. Nothing tragic.

- Skipped a day: the page rips in half dramatically, then gets taped back together when you read
- Pages logged: the pages flutter and the bookworm munches through them
- Book finished: the book slams shut in a puff of dust and the bookworm wears a graduation cap
- Yearly goal hit: books stack into a staircase and the bookworm climbs it
- Late-night reading: the bookworm in a nightcap with a tiny torch
